Full storefront work. Custom sections, the cart, collection pages, and product templates. I start from Dawn when that is the right base, and I fit the work to a custom theme when it is not.

Charm bars

This is a product, not a one-off page.

A shopper picks a chain, places charms, and can add engraving. Charm photos are cut out and saved on the product, so the builder and the product page use the same image. Checkout stops a broken set. A charm from a build cannot be bought on its own.

The builder can live in the theme or install as a Shopify app. A launch means the catalog is prepared and the storefront is wired up. You do not start from an empty theme.

CamDraft

CamDraft is a Shopify app. It drafts product descriptions and alt text from the photograph and from the facts already on the product.

The draft is meant to be found in search, quoted by an answer engine, and read by someone using a screen reader. Nothing is written back to the catalog until a person reviews it.

The App Store link will sit here when the listing is public.
