Privacy policy

This policy covers CamDraft, a Shopify app from Cam from IT. It was last updated on 22 November 2026.

Who we are

Cam from IT operates CamDraft. Questions about this policy or a request to delete app data go to support@camfromit.com.

What CamDraft stores

When a merchant installs CamDraft, the app stores the shop domain and the Shopify session it needs to call the Shopify Admin API for that shop. Session tokens stay in the app database. They are not sent to the writing models.

When a merchant runs a draft, the app stores the product facts it read and the suggestions it wrote. Those facts are the title, description, product type, tags, collections, Shopify product category, and product photos. The app also stores the brand voice, rules, and format notes the merchant writes in Writing style, plus catalog readiness counts such as whether a description is blank and whether a photo has alt text.

CamDraft does not keep a customer list. It does not ask for a shopper's name, email, address, or payment card. Shopify bills the merchant for usage. CamDraft does not store card numbers.

Who else receives product data

Generate, Regenerate, and Writing style preview send the product facts above, including up to four photos, to the model providers used to write the draft: OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic. If one provider is unavailable, another writes the draft. The app sends the product content needed for that draft. It does not send the shop's access token.

Shopify receives the usage report for a billed draft: a quantity for a blank description and a quantity for photos sent. Shopify applies the plan price. Approve and Publish do not send a usage report.

How long it is kept

The app keeps a shop's data while the app is installed. Uninstalling removes the Shopify session and marks the shop uninstalled. When Shopify sends a shop redact request, the app deletes that shop's stored data, including drafts, brand notes, catalog checks, billing records, and sessions. A customer redact request is acknowledged. CamDraft has no separate customer record to delete.

Contact

Email support@camfromit.com to ask what CamDraft holds for a shop, or to ask for it to be deleted.
